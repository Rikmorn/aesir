The layer dependency rule is: Agents -> Integrations -> Platform -> Common
- Common: imports nothing from other layers (leaf)
- Platform: imports only from Common
- Integrations: imports from Platform and Common
- Agents: imports from Integrations and Common (accesses Platform transitively)
